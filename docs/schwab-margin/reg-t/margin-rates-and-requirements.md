## Margin requirements

- To begin margin borrowing against securities in a Schwab brokerage account, you need at least \$2,000 in cash or marginable securities.3
- The amount you can borrow on margin is typically limited to 50% of the value of marginable securities in your account.
- Once you borrow on margin, you are required to maintain a certain amount of equity Tooltip
   Amount of money in an account that reflects the account holder's ownership position. 
  in your account, depending on the securities you hold.
- The typical equity maintenance requirement Tooltip
   Equity level that must be maintained in a margin account, as required by NYSE, FINRA, and individual brokerage firms. 
  is at least 25% of the total account value but can be higher for certain securities or accounts.
- Schwab calculates your buying power and cash available for withdrawal and provides the information with your Schwab brokerage account.
- In certain instances, Schwab may impose higher maintenance requirements Tooltip
   Certain stocks require a higher maintenance level. In most instances, this is set at 50%. Special maintenance requirements are set at Schwab's discretion to ensure that your account maintains sufficient equity to cover large price movements. Some factors that may contribute to a stock being subject to special maintenance requirement include, but are not limited to, volatility and liquidity.\
  \
  Also, certain accounts require a higher maintenance level. Utilizing margin in a concentrated or non-diversified portfolio may increase your risk to individual security price fluctuations. Consequently, Schwab may impose higher margin requirements on your account. Schwab reserves the right to change the margin requirements on your account at any time and without prior notice. 
  to protect clients from greater exposure to market risk.

## What is a margin call?

When the equity in your margin account drops below the minimum maintenance requirement (25% for most securities), it can trigger a margin call or margin maintenance call Tooltip   Issued when the value of margined securities plus any cash balance in an account is less than the maintenance requirement of the margined securities (typically 25% for equity securities).   . This means you'll have to deposit cash or securities into your account to meet the minimum equity requirement. Otherwise, Schwab may have to sell securities in your account at the current market value. 

View more details for Schwab clients

- Schwab may notify you of a margin call by phone, email, or mail. You can also view your alerts in the message center, which is available from the "Messages" link at the top of all pages in this website.
- You can meet a margin call promptly by depositing funds via wire, check (delivered to a branch) or MoneyLink. Or you can liquidate securities, journal cash/securities from other Schwab accounts, or deposit marginable stock.
- You are not entitled to an extension of time to meet a margin call. If you are unable to satisfy a margin maintenance call, Schwab may take the necessary steps to protect its secured loan, including selling some or all of the securities in your account.
- If your account is liquidated and a debit balance remains, you are still required to repay Schwab the amount of money you borrowed on margin, minus whatever proceeds were obtained through the liquidation. If there is a debit balance in your account, Schwab may make arrangements with you to repay the debt.
